Please clarify whether the proposed model trained on the synthetic dataset was directly evaluated on the MSU/ORNL dataset without retraining; if not, please revise the claims regarding cross-domain generalisation and model transferability.
Can the authors provide experimental results demonstrating that the proposed KS/PSI-based drift detection and shadow-retraining strategy actually detects and mitigates distribution shifts, rather than presenting it only as a proposed future framework?
Does the reported 98.1% prevention accuracy represent actual cyberattack prevention in a closed-loop smart-grid environment, or only the correctness of the policy/action mapping? Please provide closed-loop validation or clearly limit the prevention claims.
What is the experimental justification for selecting the confidence threshold τ = 0.7? Please provide a threshold-sensitivity or calibration analysis showing its effect on false blocking, missed responses, and detection performance.
How did the authors ensure that samples belonging to the same attack scenario, event, or temporal sequence were not distributed across both training and testing folds in the MSU/ORNL evaluation? Please provide scenario-, event-, or time-disjoint validation where possible.
Since the authors acknowledge that no new learning algorithm is introduced, what specific scientific novelty distinguishes this work from existing smart-grid IDS studies that already combine ensemble learning, XAI/SHAP, heterogeneous cyber-physical features, and external datasets?
How do the authors demonstrate that the SHAP explanations are faithful, rather than merely stable across folds or perturbations? Please provide a quantitative feature-removal/deletion or insertion-based faithfulness analysis, or appropriately qualify the explainability claims.
What experimental evidence explains the substantial performance difference between the synthetic dataset and the MSU/ORNL dataset, and how do the authors justify that the higher synthetic-data performance is not primarily due to an easier or more controlled data distribution?
Can the authors provide complete implementation details for the deep-learning baselines, including architecture, number of layers and parameters, optimizer, learning rate, batch size, epochs, regularisation, early stopping, and hyperparameter-selection procedure, to ensure reproducibility?
Are the software and library versions used for the original and revised experiments fully consistent? Please reconcile the reported Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, SciPy, and other relevant versions and clearly specify the final computational environment used to generate the reported results.
